# Law, tax, policy, governance

Source routes from the operator-supplied Round 2 fieldbook. These are documented
leads, not a claim that every API was tested. See [provenance](fieldbook.md) and
[catalog conventions](catalog.md); use the original documentation before collection.

| Source | Investigative opening | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Federal Register public inspection](https://www.archives.gov/federal-register/public-inspection) | See certain proposed/final federal documents before formal publication; useful early policy watch. | Public-inspection documents and final published versions may differ; never confuse proposed with effective law. |
| [Federal Register API](https://www.federalregister.gov/developers/documentation/api/v1) | Subscribe to rules by agency, topic, effective/publication date and docket. | Not a substitute for codified current requirements. |
| [GovInfo feeds & API](https://www.govinfo.gov/developers) | Follow Congressional Record, regulatory publications, budgets, legal opinions and other official collections. | Each collection's publication lag and completeness vary. |
| [Congress.gov API](https://api.congress.gov/) | Track bill changes, committee steps, amendments and associated documents. | Bill text is not law; legislative calendars are not guarantees. |
| [CourtListener / RECAP](https://free.law/projects/courtlistener/) | Search judicial opinions, dockets and tracked documents; create alerts for ongoing cases. | RECAP depends on participating users and source coverage; absence is not proof of no filing. |
| [PACER](https://pacer.uscourts.gov/) | Official U.S. federal court docket and document verification. | Fees, authentication, sealed filings and indexing delays; judicial deadline checks require attorney verification. |
| [U.S. Tax Court opinions](https://ustaxcourt.gov/find-an-opinion/) | Track holdings and fact patterns in tax disputes that may undermine a planning argument. | Precedential weight and jurisdiction/appeal status must be evaluated. |
| [IRS Internal Revenue Bulletins](https://www.irs.gov/irb) | Track official revenue procedures, revenue rulings and other tax guidance. | General announcements are not case-specific advice; effective dates are critical. |
| [OECD tax resources](https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/international-taxation.html) | Follow international policy discussions, Pillar Two implementation and cross-border comparative context. | Implementation depends on each jurisdiction's enacted law and administrative guidance. |
| [PwC Pillar Two country tracker](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/services/tax/pillar-two-readiness/country-tracker.html) | Quickly orient to jurisdiction differences and changes. | Secondary advisory summary; always verify against official local law. |

### Investigation seeds

A legal desk should monitor both **new authority** and **the status of authority it already relies on**. A case that was persuasive yesterday can change after an appellate decision or factual distinction. An alert without subsequent-treatment review is incomplete.
