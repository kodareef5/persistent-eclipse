# Cadence: an analyst's desk

Illustrative expectations, to adapt with the operator. This is guidance for PE's
manager, not a program. Draw on it when writing the lab's CADENCE record, keep the
promises the available team can meet, and use SCOPE for the report contracts and
access limits.
See [manager planning](cadence.md) for the distinction between intent and
actual execution. A different desk can use the alternatives below.

## Standing service

- Before the bell: a source-linked report on overnight developments, exposures,
  catalysts, research advanced, and uncertainty. Example handoff: 08:30
  America/New_York on agreed trading days; set the actual exchange/holiday policy.
- During the session: material-event alerts at the operator's threshold. Routine
  noise should not displace sustained investigation.
- After close: compare expectations with events and identify changed assumptions.
  Example handoff: 16:15 in the same timezone; reassess capacity by 15:40.
- Weekend: synthesize field coverage, changed theses, misses, and the coming week's
  research priorities. Agree the handoff time and protected investigation windows.

## Protected research and room to change the plan

Evenings allow quarterlies, received diligence, supply-chain research, and serious
counterarguments. Protect progress on a sustained question alongside broad coverage;
do not require a finished thesis every evening. Maps, comparisons, source dossiers,
and dissenting assessments develop in their own shapes and feed later briefings.

Capacity/models/spend and operating hours: *(agree with operator in SCOPE/LAB)*.
If a material event displaces deep work, record its restart point and when to
reassess it. A small desk can rotate responsibilities; a larger desk can maintain
several specialties. The manager decides assignments and revises the plan.

## Reasons for the manager to reassess

A reporting window approaches; a worker returns; a slot becomes available; a
filing changes a premise; a source becomes unavailable; or a blocked input arrives.
Record a concrete next trigger in QUEUE when helpful. Decide what to do after
checking current evidence and capacity. Keep missed commitments, partial coverage,
and next steps visible in STATUS.

## Adapt for another desk

| Desk | Standing service to agree | Sustained work to protect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Editorial / SEO | Daily opportunity brief, weekly planning, monthly pillar review | Community/customer research, long-tail intent, competitor analysis, supported outlines |
| Legal | Docket/authority review, weekly strategy, preparation before verified milestones | Historical precedent, adverse arguments, factual dependencies, procedural alternatives |
| Tax | Monthly operating review, quarterly planning, transaction-triggered assessment | Conditional treatments, exceptions, scenarios, multijurisdictional comparisons |

Agree a wake-up and failure-notice arrangement with the operator before promising
unattended service. Calendar expectations alone do not keep a manager running.
