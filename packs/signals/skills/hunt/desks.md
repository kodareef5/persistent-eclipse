# Four persistent research desks

A desk takes responsibility for an ongoing body of work. It starts with incomplete
coverage, learns the operator's priorities, develops specialties, and produces
useful work on a schedule. Its value should grow even when the operator supplies
no new assignments.

The examples below are imagined operating mandates, not current claims about
markets, medicine, law, or taxes. When the operator leaves direction open, they
show good shapes for the work; draw on them rather than reproducing one. Models and staffing are operator choices. Two
modest workers can serve a narrow desk; more capacity can support broader coverage,
parallel investigations, and multiple cooperating labs. Neither scale guarantees
good judgment.

For three additional source-rich sketches—life sciences, industrial systems, and
open technology—see [specialist desks](desks.specialist.md). For fictional examples
of finished work, see [output specimens](output.specimens.md). pe-osint's fieldbook
supplies the professional tools, published investigations, and source routes.

## Financial intelligence: an analyst's research desk

An analyst arrives before the opening bell to a report that connects overnight
developments to the exposures, watchlist, and questions they care about. The desk
has scanned markets and sectors, distinguished changed assumptions from familiar
headlines, and identified what deserves attention today. It knows the analyst's
horizon and preferences: an interesting development is not automatically relevant
to this portfolio or mandate.

During the session it watches material disclosures and catalysts. The closing
review revisits the morning's expectations and flags surprises. Evening work
examines quarterlies, management claims, customers, competitors, and diligence
received from other labs. Weekend research makes room for sector maps, historical
comparisons, difficult countertheses, and connections outside the immediate
watchlist. Imported diligence is a starting point to challenge, not a shortcut to
confidence.

An early rare-earths lead might grow into a six-week investigation of processing
capacity, permitting, trade constraints, financing, and downstream buyers. A
medication story might lead from trial disclosures into manufacturing, coverage
policies, medical administration, distribution, and customer behavior. The desk
asks which links are real, where timing breaks the proposed mechanism, what others
already expect, and who could benefit under different scenarios. It can conclude
that a compelling story creates no assessable opportunity.

These become specialties inside a broad research program. They do not replace
coverage of the rest of the field. After three months, the analyst should receive
better questions, faster context on surprises, and clearer confidence updates,
not just a larger news digest.

### How the desk develops

Illustrative progression, not financial findings:

- **Day 1:** start with a watchlist, filings, market news, a small source map, and
  the promised briefings. State gaps instead of pretending to cover every sector.
- **Week 2:** related disclosures suggest a rare-earth processing constraint worth
  sustained work. Follow financing, buyers, competing capacity, and alternative
  explanations. The broad market watch and morning service continue.
- **Month 2:** new filings weaken the original beneficiary thesis but strengthen
  another. The desk now has a source network, relationship map, comparisons, and
  explicit conditions for revisiting both views. Briefings link to this developing
  work; they do not reproduce it or hide its unresolved questions.

**Source route to try:** connect [USGS capacity baselines](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/mineral-commodity-summaries),
[USITC trade data](https://www.usitc.gov/applications/dataweb/about),
[PortWatch](https://portwatch.imf.org/), and the company's own filings. Reconcile
physical constraints with company economics, as [SemiAnalysis describes](https://semianalysis.com/core-research/).
A correct industry forecast still need not imply a mispriced security.

**Start here:** [market-watch scope](scope.market-watch.md).
It offers pre-market, intraday, closing, evening, and weekend rhythms, with a
bounded watchlist and explicit protection for deeper diligence.

## Editorial intelligence and SEO: an opportunity desk

A small company publishing perhaps one article a day might dedicate two economical
researchers to its niche. Its morning report should explain what they did to find
worthwhile opportunities: which conversations changed, what customer questions
kept recurring, what competitors missed, and which existing pages need attention.
Some mornings the best answer is to improve an existing explanation or investigate
a promising question further.

The research gradually reveals the audience's language, tasks, uncertainties, and
decisions. Community interactions supply leads and long-tail phrasing. Customer
analysis tests whether those needs fit the company's audience. Competitor and
search research reveal effective concepts, neglected intent, and gaps that the
company can credibly address. The desk builds outlines and content-pillar proposals
around that understanding, with limited samples when useful. It can recommend a
community interaction without making the post itself.

At a large publisher, the same responsibility spans beats: technology, consumer
behavior, health, culture, business. Some capacity follows timely developments;
other researchers investigate emerging categories, underserved audiences, evergreen
questions, and relationships between topics. A change in AI hardware could suggest
news, a comparison, a buying-guide concept, and a longer-running editorial program.
Those options compete for resources. The desk should explain their relationship,
audience value, required original work, and reasons to decline them.

The scale changes the breadth and depth, not the purpose. A narrow company desk
and a large editorial department both need original judgment, a plan beyond the
next story, and feedback about which research proved useful. Publishing more
interchangeable posts is not evidence of progress.

**Source route to try:** compare original questions in specialist communities with
[Media Cloud coverage](https://www.mediacloud.org/media-cloud-search),
[Search Console data](https://developers.google.com/webmaster-tools/v1/how-tos/search_analytics)
for an authorized property, and the actual reader problem. [Data Is Plural](https://www.data-is-plural.com/archive/)
can suggest original datasets for reporting. A popular query alone does not earn an article.

**Start here:** [editorial scope](scope.tech-editorial.md).
It pairs daily opportunities with weekly planning and monthly audience, competitor,
and pillar reviews, scaled to the operator's resources.

## Legal research: a continuing case desk

A litigation team gives a small desk responsibility for supporting a matter over
its life. It first reconstructs the parties, claims, factual disputes, procedural
history, jurisdictions, authorities, and competing positions. It learns what the
attorneys need to decide and where the available record is incomplete.

Daily work follows relevant developments and checks whether pending arguments or
procedural preparations need attention. Before a hearing or filing, the desk
prepares focused research and a procedural checklist for attorney review. It looks
both for risks of a procedural mistake and for supportable procedural opportunities
in the opposing position. Each checklist item needs a relevant source and an
explicit applicability check; a generic list can give false confidence.

Quiet periods are research time. One line of inquiry develops the strongest
support for a position; another tries to dismantle it. Historical precedent,
adverse treatment, overlooked distinctions, factual prerequisites, and alternative
strategies deserve sustained attention. A new fact can overturn a promising
argument, while an unfavorable decision can suggest a narrower route. The useful
record preserves what failed and why, so the team does not repeatedly rediscover
the same weakness.

A firm may dedicate two to five workers to one matter, or support several desks
with shared methods and permitted authority research. Staffing does not remove
matter boundaries. Reusable search techniques can travel more freely than client
facts or strategy. The desk's daily value includes identifying that nothing
material changed, while its longer investigations continue advancing the case.

**Source route to try:** follow a relied-upon proposition through
[CourtListener](https://free.law/projects/courtlistener/) and the official docket,
then compare any new authority with the facts and posture that made the original
argument work. A missing RECAP entry does not establish that nothing was filed.

**Start here:** [legal scope](scope.legal-strategy.md).
It combines daily awareness, weekly strategy, historical and adverse research, and
preparation before independently verified procedural milestones.

## Tax intelligence: operating continuity and strategic research

For a small business, a desk can support the accountant alongside recurring
bookkeeping. Within explicitly authorized access, it learns the entity structure,
recurring transactions, seasonal patterns, unresolved reconciliations, and prior
planning decisions. Monthly work identifies inconsistencies and questions worth
resolving; quarterly reviews connect that operating picture to upcoming decisions
and changing guidance. It prepares review material rather than silently changing
the books or assuming a treatment applies.

An angel investor or acquisitive business has a different research agenda:
structures, financing, capital transactions, exits, alternative sequences, and
jurisdictional questions. The desk compares scenarios and hunts for the exception
or missing fact that would defeat an attractive idea. At enterprise scale, separate
research lines may examine international structures, intercompany relationships,
regulatory changes, and competing interpretations, then reconcile their
assumptions before presenting a combined assessment.

Reactive work and strategic work coexist. A new transaction or official publication
may require a prompt impact review. In between, a months-long investigation can
compare alternatives before the operator commits. A calculation may sharpen a
question without settling eligibility. A subtle exception that rules out a favored
strategy is useful knowledge, including the conditions under which it might be
worth reconsidering.

The desk scales from a modest recurring service to specialized research capacity.
Its continuity should reduce repeated work, improve the questions put to qualified
reviewers, and make uncertainty and documentation needs visible early.

**Start here:** [tax scope](scope.tax-research.md).
It distinguishes monthly operating support, investor planning, and enterprise
research, with scope-specific records, review windows, and access boundaries.

**Source route to try:** trace a changed treatment from an
[IRS bulletin](https://www.irs.gov/irb) or [Tax Court opinion](https://ustaxcourt.gov/find-an-opinion/)
to its effective period and factual prerequisites. For cross-border questions,
use [OECD context](https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/international-taxation.html) to
find the question, then verify the local authority. Preserve the strongest adverse
interpretation for the professional reviewing the work.

## Shape the service with the operator

| Operator sets | Desk develops and proposes |
| --- | --- |
| Beneficiary, objectives, constraints, permitted inputs | Research agenda, maps of the field, specialties and blind spots |
| Staffing, models, spend, operating hours | Allocation between standing service, deep work, and better methods |
| Reporting windows and materiality thresholds | Monitoring coverage, source selection, useful changes to cadence |
| Deliverable expectations and review criteria | Clearer briefs, stronger outlines, competing theses, usable negative findings |

Begin with a credible service at the available scale. Maintain awareness across
the agreed field, protect sustained investigations, and improve the lab's methods
and source knowledge. If a new specialty consumes the desk, restore coverage or
propose a scope change. If reporting consumes everything, simplify the report and
protect a smaller deep-research commitment.

See [starting a desk](starting.md) for useful capabilities to develop as needed.
pe-forecast's HN note illustrates how a simple proxy can mislead and how to design a
more useful observation. PE and the [record mapping](records.md) handle the research
record.
