# Scope: Tax research

Support continuing operating or strategic tax research by tracing authority,
factual requirements, exceptions, and timing across recurring decisions. Draw on it
when drafting `.research/SCOPE.md`; keep what fits the operator's request and fill in
what they actually asked for. This template supplies no tax
treatment or filing advice.

- Service level: small-business operating support, investor planning, or enterprise

Identify facts and documentation needed for application without assuming they are
available. Public-source research is the default. Operator-authorized accounting
or tax records stay within the designated workspace and handling boundaries; their
availability must be explicit. Do not export them into shared resource knowledge.

## Investigation

Trace the primary authority, effective dates, applicability, exceptions, and
contrary interpretations. Distinguish proposed, final, and effective provisions;
publication date alone does not determine applicability to a period or transaction.
Check whether the idea depends on an election, transaction order, status, or factual
threshold. Compare alternatives under the stated facts and show where the answer
changes if an assumption changes. Save enough of the authority to inspect the
reasoning later, and mark unavailable checks explicitly.

## Deliverables and progress

A conditional comparison or research memo with supporting and adverse authority,
assumptions, possible disqualifiers, required facts/documentation, timing questions,
and issues for a qualified reviewer. A calculation, if useful, retains its inputs,
method, and limits in a REQ run. An estimate does not establish eligibility.

Progress includes ruling out an attractive idea, discovering an exception, or
isolating the missing fact that decides between treatments. No forecast is required
for a question about applicability.

## Service profile

Choose a service level and protect sustained comparisons alongside immediate needs.

| Mandate | Recurring service | Sustained investigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small business | Monthly bookkeeping-support review and questions for the accountant | Recurring transactions, documentation, conditional alternatives |
| Investor / growing business | Quarterly planning and transaction-triggered review | Entities, capital transactions, exits, timing, exceptions |
| International enterprise | Regulatory and transaction impact reviews | Intercompany relationships, competing treatments, scenario comparisons |

Annual strategy can connect these windows. If daily updates are needed, report
changes and research progress; do not invent a strategy to fill the page.

## Vocabulary and first project

Entity, jurisdiction, period, transaction, sequence, treatment, exception, election,
effective date, scenario, authority. Identify recurring questions and official
source changes affecting this business. Reconcile authorized inputs before adding
scenario calculations. At larger scale, compare assumptions across research lines
before combining results. See [conditional investigations](plays.md).

Review questions resolved for the professional reviewer, inconsistent inputs found,
alternatives ruled out, and repeated work avoided. Reuse public-source methods
separately from private records and client analysis.

## Report contract

Deliver an operating review after each monthly records cutoff at *(edit: handoff
and timezone)*, with quarterly planning and transaction reviews as agreed above.
Save to `.research/reports/tax/<entity-id>/<YYYY-MM-DD>/<report-id>-review.md`.
Choose a non-sensitive entity identifier. Use these headings:

```markdown
# Tax research review
## Period, entities, jurisdictions, and records cutoff
## Reconciled inputs, discrepancies, and missing documentation
## Guidance changes and conditional applicability
## Alternatives, calculations, exceptions, and contrary authority
## Timing questions and decisions for the qualified reviewer

```

Agree separate short formats for quarterly and annual service reviews; save them
as `<report-id>-quarterly.md` or `-annual.md`, with their window and links to the
work beneath the assessment. Distinguish source publication/effective dates,
transaction dates, verified external deadlines, and research targets.

## Sustained investigation artifacts

Transaction studies, scenario models, authority comparisons, and alternative entity
structures choose the form needed to explain their dependencies. Keep reviewed work
under `.research/reports/tax/<entity-id>/investigations/<question>/`; set checkpoints
and evidence/review criteria with the qualified reviewer. Retain calculation inputs,
methods, applicability tests, exceptions, and contrary interpretations. Recurring
briefs report changes without compressing this work into their skeleton. Keep
uncertain eligibility and unavailable checks visible; an estimate does not determine
a treatment.

## Authorization

Research within the authorized source and record boundaries. No book amendments,
returns, elections, filings, transactions, or external communications. Surface unresolved questions
for the operator's tax reviewer.
