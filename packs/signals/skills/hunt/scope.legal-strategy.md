# Scope: Legal strategy research

Maintain a case desk: daily awareness, procedural preparation, historical and
adverse research, and support for evolving strategy. Draw on it when drafting
`.research/SCOPE.md`; keep what fits the operator's request and fill in what they
actually asked for.
This template is a research brief; it supplies no substantive legal conclusions.

mapping and other work that does not depend on that answer.

## Investigation

Map the position into propositions, factual prerequisites, supporting authorities,
and strongest opposing arguments. Check the authority's text, jurisdiction,
status, subsequent treatment, and relevance to these facts. Separate binding and
persuasive authority and distinguish a holding from analogy or commentary. Record
an unavailable status check as a limitation, not as confirmation that authority
remains good. A failed search is not proof that no adverse authority exists.

Give serious effort to tearing down the favored position. Preserve a narrower
argument or an alternative route when the main one fails. Coverage belongs in
FRONTIER: issue, sources searched, search date, limits, and reasons to return.

## Deliverables and progress

An argument memo or issue map: proposition, supporting and adverse authority,
factual application, alternatives, decisive uncertainty, and next research step.
Keep citations traceable to saved extracts and precise sections or passages.
A finding that the position fails can be the most useful result. Do not equate
completion of a memo with validation of its conclusion.

## Service profile

Start with one matter and perhaps two to five workers. A smaller desk can rotate
methods; larger firms can run separate matter desks with permitted sharing.

| Window | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Daily | Relevant docket/authority changes and procedural preparation risks |
| Material event | Reassess affected arguments and factual assumptions |
| Protected blocks | Historical precedent, strongest support, adverse research, alternative strategies |
| Weekly | What improved, failed, or now depends on a missing fact |
| Before verified milestones | Focused memo and procedural checklist for attorney review |
Prepare source-linked checklists for attorney review; examine procedural risks
and opportunities on both sides without assuming an apparent defect is actionable.

## Vocabulary and first project

Matter, issue, proposition, authority, factual predicate, procedural event, adverse
argument, alternative, deadline. Index official opinions, rules, dockets, historical
authority, permitted research services, and authorized matter documents around
named propositions. Add targeted change alerts and subsequent-treatment checks
with suitable access. A quiet docket leaves room for older precedent and adversarial
research. See [argument investigations](plays.md).

A checklist records the applicable rule/version, triggering fact, required step,
verified timing, reviewer, and unresolved question. Review attorney usefulness,
errors caught, arguments narrowed, and overlooked counterarguments. Transfer
methods across matters only within the sharing boundaries above.

## Report contract

Deliver a daily case update at *(edit: time and timezone)*, covering changes since
the prior cutoff. Agree distinct short briefs for weekly strategy and verified
milestone reviews.
Save to `.research/reports/legal/<matter-id>/<YYYY-MM-DD>/<report-id>-case.md`.
Choose a non-sensitive matter identifier. Use these headings:

```markdown
# Case research update
## Docket and authority changes through the cutoff
## Argument map: support, adverse authority, factual dependencies
## Procedural checklist: risks, openings, and verified timing
## Research advanced, failed routes, and alternatives
## Attorney review questions and next investigation
```

State the cutoff and unavailable checks explicitly. Cite precise passages in saved
extracts. Keep weekly/milestone briefs in the same folder as `<report-id>-strategy.md`
or `<report-id>-milestone.md`, following their agreed sections and linking to the
underlying argument research. Research completion does not establish a legal deadline.

## Sustained investigation artifacts

A historical-authority dossier, argument map, procedural-options memo, or adversarial
analysis should follow the question. Preserve reviewed work under
`.research/reports/legal/<matter-id>/investigations/<issue>/` and agree checkpoints
with the attorney reviewer. Test factual predicates, applicability, subsequent
treatment, and the strongest contrary authority. Mark incomplete checks; a daily
update should not truncate the argument or turn a finished memo into a validated
position. Carry failed routes forward so later work does not repeat them.

## Authorization

Research within the operator's authorized source and matter boundaries. No filings,
submissions, contact with parties or counsel, or commitments on the operator's
behalf. Identify the unresolved issues for the operator's legal reviewer.
