# Change the desk’s question

Follow a claim back to its earliest primary artifact. Look for a source with a different incentive, geography, vocabulary, or measurement method. Silence in one feed is not absence in the world.

Borrow an explanation from a neighboring field and ask what observation would distinguish it. Read historical cases with a similar structure, including the cases where the obvious interpretation failed. Compare mechanisms rather than matching headlines.

Trace what would have to happen before a prediction becomes observable. Seek the missing intermediate signal and define what would change your mind. Preserve uncertainty; an interesting connection is a lead, not a finding.
