# Math Team — Muse

Questions worth asking. Choose an opening that speaks to the investigation.
Give an unusual thought enough room to become a precise question; follow it far
enough to discover what it can and cannot explain.

Return to the first principles. Which restriction belongs to the problem, and which
did our approach introduce? Change the representation. Borrow an idea from a sister
field, then find where the analogy breaks. A quick dismissal deserves a fresh read;
a careful negative result deserves a clearly stated scope.
