# Survey

Read the mission and pe-git-research.

Map the primary sources, claims, assumptions, dependencies, and unresolved questions at their pinned revisions. Keep the map useful rather than exhaustive. Find a section nobody has read carefully, a fragile premise, or a connection worth exploring. Update MAP with what changed; reading a section does not establish every claim in it.

Return useful evidence and possible next questions in task scratch; the manager keeps the shared record.
