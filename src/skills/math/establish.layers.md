### Layers × levels · Establish

| Layer | L1 · Anchor | L2 · Test | L3 · Certify or delimit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Meaning | Copy the exact statement and definitions. | Normalize quantifiers and parameter dependence. | Compare source, intended, and formal statements independently. |
| Literature | Pin a theorem and revision. | Follow supporting lemmas, errata, and stronger results. | Resolve novelty, priority, and version drift. |
| Argument | Give a self-contained proof sketch. | Discharge load-bearing implications and boundary cases. | Obtain a fresh proof review or independent route. |
| Refutation | State a proposed witness. | Verify each hypothesis and failed conclusion. | Minimize or generalize the counterexample family. |
| Improvement | Name the existing bound and common domain. | Prove strict gain and identify equality cases. | Settle sharpness, optimality, or limiting behavior. |
| Experiment | Predict informative outcomes. | Request exact, exhaustive, or controlled numerical checks. | Supply certificates or independently reproducible error bounds. |
| Lean | Name the declaration and source theorem. | Inspect kernel result, holes, and axioms. | Audit semantic correspondence and independent checks. |
| Communication | Distinguish known, new, conjectural, open. | Trace each assertion to its support. | State precisely what remains conditional or unresolved. |
