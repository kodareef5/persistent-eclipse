### Layers × levels · Explore

| Layer | L1 · See | L2 · Relate | L3 · Extend |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Statement | Rewrite the claim plainly. | Expose binders, domains, and hidden hypotheses. | Find the strongest plausible true form or weakest needed assumptions. |
| Examples | Try zero, one, tiny, symmetric cases. | Organize examples into families. | Find an extremal or separating construction. |
| Structure | Spot invariants, symmetries, monotonicity. | Translate to an equivalent formulation. | Identify a conserved quantity, dual object, or obstruction. |
| Parameters | Mark ranges and endpoints. | Track which constants depend on which parameters. | Seek sharp thresholds, optimal rates, or uniformity. |
| Models | Build a toy model. | Compare discrete/continuous or finite/infinite versions. | Prove a transfer step—or find why it fails. |
| Connections | Recall a related lemma. | Relate by reduction, analogy, or duality. | Import a method from another field with its hypotheses intact. |
| Conjectures | Form one falsifiable guess. | Branch into stronger, weaker, and conditional variants. | Explain competing mechanisms or a conjecture lattice. |
| Literature | Find the closest source theorem. | Compare versions, assumptions, and normalizations. | Locate the genuine frontier, missing case, or prior art. |
| Proof kernel | Name the hard step. | Split it into lemmas and alternatives. | Reduce it to the smallest decisive implication. |
