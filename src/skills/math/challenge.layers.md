### Layers × levels · Challenge

| Layer | L1 · Poke | L2 · Stress | L3 · Break or isolate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Statement | Negate the exact conclusion. | Separate universal, existential, and uniform claims. | Build a witness satisfying every assumption. |
| Examples | Check trivial and equality cases. | Search extremal, random, and pathological families. | Produce a minimal counterexample or impossibility mechanism. |
| Parameters | Check endpoints. | Vary scales, signs, rates, and constant dependencies. | Locate a sharp failure threshold. |
| Proof | Circle each nontrivial implication. | Trace the load-bearing lemma's dependencies. | Rebuild its proof independently or exhibit the exact gap. |
| Techniques | Test algebra and inequalities. | Change normalization or approach through a dual formulation. | Expose a missing compactness, uniformity, or transfer step. |
| Sources | Check the cited theorem's hypotheses. | Reconcile version and notation changes. | Find an erratum, countertheorem, or unproved imported dependency. |
| Formalization | Read the Lean statement. | Compare binders, types, and coercions to the intended claim. | Find vacuity, an added axiom, or a changed theorem hidden by success. |
| Novelty | Look for a named antecedent. | Search equivalent forms and stronger known results. | Determine whether the claimed improvement is genuinely strict. |
