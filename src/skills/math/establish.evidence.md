### What the evidence means · Establish

| Evidence | Supports | Does not by itself support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source citation | A pinned authorial statement or published proof. | Correctness, originality, or absence of later errata. |
| Informal derivation | A mathematical argument with inspectable steps. | Correctness of an unproved load-bearing lemma. |
| Independent review | A second assessment of exact assumptions and critical seams. | Formal or exhaustive verification. |
| Explicit counterexample | Refutation of a universal claim if all assumptions and failure are checked. | Refutation of a different or weakened statement. |
| Exact symbolic identity | Equality within the stated algebraic assumptions. | Unstated branch, domain, or nonzero-denominator conditions. |
| Exhaustive finite search | Coverage of the enumerated domain and constraints. | A universal result outside that domain. |
| Randomized experiment | A pattern, possible obstruction, or statistical estimate. | A theorem without a justified probabilistic argument. |
| Interval / certified numerics | A stated bound within rounding and error assumptions. | A result outside the certified interval or model. |
| Lean kernel check | The named formal proposition under its axioms. | Faithfulness to the intended claim or freedom from custom axioms. |
| Failed run / tactic | A specific attempted method did not complete. | Mathematical falsity, impossibility, or nonexistence. |
